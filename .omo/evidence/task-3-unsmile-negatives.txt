# KoJev: UnSmile nouls could only ever be answered 예

## Measured defect

    train: unsmile NOUL gold distribution = {1: 8610}
    val:   unsmile NOUL gold distribution = {1: 519}

9,129 binary questions whose honest answer was always 예. Nouls were emitted
only for ACTIVE categories, so no category a sentence did NOT belong to was ever
asked about.

The CHOICE questions from the same source were healthy, spread across all nine
options:

    train: unsmile CHOICE gold = {0:679, 1:620, 2:515, 3:685, 4:227, 5:443,
                                  6:438, 7:174, 8:1713}

That mix is why the source-level totals never looked obviously broken, and why
the defect had to be found by splitting the distribution BY QUESTION TYPE.

## How it was found

smilegate-ai/kor_unsmile scored 0.1246 against a 0.6426 majority and stayed
there through every device, dtype, sampling and corpus fix. A 52-point deficit
that survives six unrelated fixes is a property of the data, not the model.

## Fix

Matched negatives: for each row, as many inactive categories as active ones are
asked with gold=0. `_pick_negatives` keys the choice on a SHA-256 of the state
text, so the selection is stable across rebuilds and varies between rows instead
of always naming the first categories in the list.

## RED

    E   AssertionError: every noul is answerable 예; the sub-task is degenerate
    E   assert 0 in {1}

## An intentional contract change

`test_unsmile_emits_one_noul_per_category_and_single_label_choice` asserted
exactly two questions. Adding negatives makes that three. The test was updated
to the new contract and now asserts the noul golds are exactly {0, 1}. This is a
deliberate behaviour change, not a suppressed failure.
