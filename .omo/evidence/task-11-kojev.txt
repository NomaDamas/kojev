
## Follow-up wave-scheduling fix and scale-up

The original runner defect was one task per candidate with a semaphore inside the task, leaving thousands of tasks queued inside the teacher client's fixed 90-second timeout. The fix in `kojev/label.py` is:

- `WAVE_SIZE = INITIAL_CONCURRENCY` (32).
- `LabelRunner.run` slices candidates into explicit 32-item waves and awaits `_run_wave` before creating the next wave.
- Each completed wave is appended immediately, preserving restart durability.
- Each item retries timeout failures twice; exhausted timeouts are skipped without aborting the wave or overall run.
- `teacher.py` and all other forbidden files remain unmodified.

New deterministic tests prove wave peak concurrency and timeout retry behavior.
