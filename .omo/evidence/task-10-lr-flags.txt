# KoJev: the learning rates were reportable but not settable

Job 13635 died in 11 seconds:

    train.py: error: unrecognized arguments: --backbone-lr 1e-5

backbone_lr and head_lr were already TrainConfig fields, already threaded through
run_training, and already written into report.json. Only argparse never exposed
them. The plan's escalation rule is to retry a diverged seed at a LOWER learning
rate, so without these flags that rule could not be carried out at all.

This is the same shape of gap as the missing majority baseline: the capability
existed everywhere except the one place the work needed to reach it, which is
why reading report.json made the knob look available.

## RED

    kojev.train: error: unrecognized arguments: --backbone-lr 1e-5 --head-lr 5e-4
    E   SystemExit: 2

## Fix

--backbone-lr and --head-lr added, defaulting to the existing 2e-5 and 1e-3 so
current behaviour is unchanged, and passed through main() to run_training.

## Real surface

    $ python -m kojev.train --help
      --backbone-lr BACKBONE_LR
      --head-lr HEAD_LR
