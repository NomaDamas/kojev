DESIGN DECISIONS
- BundleError is a PLAIN ValueError subclass, not a frozen dataclass. Todo 9
  proved frozen dataclass exceptions reject __traceback__ assignment and surface
  as TypeError; that pitfall is deliberately not repeated here.
- Error messages are built by classmethods on BundleError, matching the existing
  SchemaError convention in kojev/schema.py and satisfying TRY003/EM102 without
  suppressions.
- The argparse boundary is read through vars() cast to dict[str, object] and
  narrowed by isinstance, so no `# pyright: ignore[reportAny]` is needed.
- Manifest ordering is deterministic (sorted paths) so bundles are reproducible.
