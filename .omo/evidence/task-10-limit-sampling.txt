# KoJev: --limit trained and evaluated on different distributions

Four hyperparameter configurations all plateaued at the same ~0.031 margin:

| batch | backbone lr | steps | diverged | gate  |
|-------|-------------|-------|----------|-------|
| 4     | 2e-5        | 500   | True     | n/a   |
| 4     | 1e-5        | 500   | True     | -0.071|
| 8     | 2e-5        | 250   | True     | 0.0311|
| 16    | 1e-5        | 125   | False    | 0.0315|
| 16    | 2e-5        | 125   | False    | 0.0331|

That flatness across a 4x range of step counts and two learning rates was the
real signal: the limiter was not optimisation.

## Root cause

`--limit` took a HEAD SLICE of a corpus that the builder writes source by
source, so it selected whole sources and omitted the rest.

Measured on the real corpus:

  first 2000 TRAIN rows: 7 of 12 sources
    lawcompany/KLAID          33.2%   (7.7% in the full corpus, inflated 4.3x)
    searle-j/kote             18.1%
    e9t/nsmc                  15.2%
    KorQuAD/squad_kor_v1       ABSENT (15.4% of the full corpus, the largest)
    kakaobrain/kor_nli:snli    ABSENT
    kakaobrain/kor_nli:multi   ABSENT
    klue/klue:nli              ABSENT
    wicho/kor_3i4k             ABSENT

  first 2000 VAL rows: a DIFFERENT distribution
    lawcompany/KLAID           3.0%   (33.2% in the training slice)
    wicho/kor_3i4k            11.8%   (absent from the training slice)

So the smoke trained on one distribution and was scored on another, including a
source it had never seen. smilegate-ai/kor_unsmile scoring 0.12 against a 0.636
majority was the visible symptom: not undertraining, but evaluation on material
the training slice under-represented.

## Fix

`_sample` takes `limit` examples spread across the corpus using a seeded
shuffle, so the subset stays representative and the run stays reproducible.
`data_counts` now also reports `train_sources` and `val_sources`, so a skewed
subset is visible in every future report instead of having to be discovered.

## Non-tautology proof

Restoring the head slice fails the new test:

    E   AssertionError: limit covered only 3 of 12 sources
    E   assert 3 >= 10

Gates: 131 passed, ruff clean, format clean, basedpyright 0 errors.
