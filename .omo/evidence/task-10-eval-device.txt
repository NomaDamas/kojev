# KoJev: evaluation left its batches on the CPU

Job 13632 FAILED in 00:00:46 with:

    RuntimeError: Expected all tensors to be on the same device, but got index
    is on cpu, different from other tensors on cuda:0

The training loop moved its batches to the GPU, but _collect collates FRESH
batches for validation and left them on CPU. Fixing the training loop alone was
not enough: the same device discipline has to hold everywhere a batch is made.

Fix: model_device(model) derives the device from the model's own parameters, and
_collect moves each evaluation batch onto it. Deriving rather than threading the
device keeps the two paths from drifting apart again.

Non-tautology proof: reverting the eval-path move fails the new test.

    E   AssertionError: evaluation never moved a batch: 1 moves
    E   assert 1 > 1

The count works because a 2-example run performs a single optimizer step, so any
move beyond the first must have come from evaluation.
